# Sample source

The invoice files in this directory are unmodified copies of the public LHDN MyInvois SDK samples.

Source: https://sdk.myinvois.hasil.gov.my/

They were taken from a local mirror of those public samples: Invoice 1.0 and 1.1, in JSON and XML, including the consolidated, foreign-currency, and multi-line examples. The files contain placeholder party identifiers such as `Supplier's TIN` and `Buyer's TIN`. They are not real taxpayer data.

The built-in emulator tenants use those same placeholder strings, plus synthetic TINs such as `C00000000000`, so a sample can be submitted without editing it. See `fixtures/tenants.json`.
